# Groups Tab Overview

The **Groups** tab allows you to assign names to the **26 channel groups**, labeled **A through Z**. This tab is the RMS equivalent of the radio-side **Group Name** menu function.  
Each group is displayed alongside a text input field. Enter the desired name into the field to assign or change the name for that group.  
Group names may be **up to 12 characters** in length.  
See: [Main Menu → CMS → Group Name](https://github.com/nicsure/RMS880/wiki/CMS-Menu#group-name)

---

## Codeplug Inclusion

Group name settings are included when creating or loading a **Codeplug**, allowing them to be backed up, shared, or transferred together with other radio configuration data.

See: [RMS → Codeplug](https://github.com/nicsure/RMS880/wiki/Codeplug-Tab)
